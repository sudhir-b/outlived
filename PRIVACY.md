# Outlasted privacy policy

Last updated 12 September 2026.

Outlasted does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **Your date of birth and the people you pick stay on your phone.** They are saved in the app's own local storage and are never sent anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.
- **Notifications are scheduled on your phone by the app itself.** No push notification service is involved.
- **Calendar export** creates a calendar file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. The app does not know or record what you do with it.
- **Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.**

The list of famous people is built from public data published by [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org) (CC0) and the [Pantheon](https://pantheon.world) project at MIT (CC BY 4.0). Neither is contacted by the app; the list ships inside it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/sudhir-b/outlived/issues.
